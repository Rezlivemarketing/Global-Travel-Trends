# Expanded country research — 2 November 2026

The expanded edition adds official event-organiser, national-tourism, government, Eurostat, World Bank and UN Tourism evidence. The research files retain source URLs, source periods, definitions, date confidence and exclusions. No index can establish an exhaustive inventory of every local event worldwide.

Events are classified by their evidence: a published 2026 programme; a fixed annual observance with local details to confirm; or a seasonal/previous-edition record with no verified 2026 day. Undated programmes stay off the calendar grid. Explicit opening-day arrays take precedence over the outer start/end range.

The source-market expansion includes Eurostat 2024 accommodation-arrival and overnight-trip tables. Leading separately reported countries are not an exhaustive worldwide ranking because some countries are grouped within aggregates. The existing Spain FRONTUR and Great Britain ONS evidence is preserved when merging, and different directions retain their own sources and definitions.

United States and Mauritius have verified historical November/December 2024 records. These are historical seasonal context, not future 2026 travel measurements. Other source-market profiles can cover annual or other historical monthly periods. Mauritius resident departure data use country of disembarkation, which can be a transit point.

Archived World Bank/UN Tourism indicators add annual arrivals, departures, receipts and expenditure for the countries with reported values. The archive generally ends in 2020; some last observations are older. Pandemic-year observations are identified, and 2019 is shown separately. Archive totals must not be combined with recent bilateral profiles as if their coverage or periods matched.

Cancelled or postponed programmes such as the 2026 Red Sea Film Festival and Malawi Tumaini Festival are excluded. Remaining missing-country and missing-direction states indicate evidence gaps, not zero travel or proof that no local events exist. Country-by-country coverage is published in `dist/data/coverage.json`.
